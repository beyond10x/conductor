## What

axe-core reports `link-in-text-block` (WCAG 1.4.1, impact serious) in both themes at both gating
viewports:

- the overview, loaded: the pull-request number link in "Waiting for review"
  (`src/views/overview/WaitingForReview.vue`), `a[rel=noreferrer]` inside the line;
- the Sessions page, loaded and empty: the two links of the "Red CI on main is on the Overview;
  disk waste is on Health" note (`.moved > a`, `src/App.vue`).

The stylesheet removes the underline from every link (`src/style.css`, `a { text-decoration:
none }`) and the link colour against the surrounding text is below 3:1, so a reader who does not
see the colour difference cannot find the link.

## Why S2

It fails hard gate 5 for one class of user; no screen-only task needs these links, so no task is
blocked (not S1).

## Fix (one line)

Underline links that sit inside running text (or every link in `main` outside the navigation).
Cost S.
