## Why

Observed in a live run: 4 of 11 controllers reached the 300k-token hand-over in 3 to 4 h. Their
start-of-session reading was about 90k characters: the user-level instructions file, the workspace
and repository `AGENTS.md`, the charter and the controller profile.

## Acceptance

The controller profile and a charter template together are under 8k chars; the profile is split
into what a controller needs at start and a reference it reads when a step needs it.
