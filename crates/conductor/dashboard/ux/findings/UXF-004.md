The type scale (src/style.css) sets small text at 11, 12 and 13 px (`--text-xs`, `--text-sm`,
`--text-md`), and the pages use it for key data: the snapshot ages, the KPI sources, the session and
decision meta lines, the table cells. 36 of the 42 state cases at each viewport hold some text in
`main` under 14 px; the 6 that do not are health/loading, health/error and repository/loading, in
both themes.

Sessions affected: all 5 scenarios. Cost: M (a type-scale change across every page; it needs a
design decision on which data is key). Measured only, not gated.
