The navbar holds an empty `navbarSearchContainer` and no search control on any of the 36 pages. The
always-visible list for a docs site names search. With 35 pages and a sidebar of 40 entries, a reader
who does not know the page name has no way to ask the site.

Cost: M (a search plugin and its build step). Needs a design decision: see `ux/rounds/R-01/decision.md`.
