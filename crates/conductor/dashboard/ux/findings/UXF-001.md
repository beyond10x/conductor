`header.top` is not sticky (src/style.css). On the Decisions page the operator answers a request
lower down and presses Send; the page is then scrolled to the button and the result, and the header,
the instance selector and the page navigation are above the viewport, at every viewport measured
(1440x900 down to 390x844), in both themes. Every other scenario state is read at the top of the
page and holds the header in view.

Sessions affected: the decisions scenario, 1 of 5 (an anchor). Cost: M (a sticky header that does
not cover the content it scrolls to, and stays short at 390 px; a layout change, so a design
decision of its round). The baseline holds the 6 under decision R-01.
